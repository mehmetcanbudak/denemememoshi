## 5. Native-fit rules for any selection

These are applications of existing contracts, not a new architecture:

1. **Preserve the authority boundary.** Retained presentation may consume Memoshi values and issue typed intents; it must not introduce a second engine/store writer, OpenCode-compatible authority or an implicit approval path. MemoshiProtocol/Pi Durable, policy, recovery and device contracts remain authoritative. ([D91][D91], [earlier coupling assessment][R-REUSE].)
2. **Adapt navigation, not the domain.** Mac can expose workbench depth beside the conversation; iPhone uses the approved compact navigation/presentation; iPad adapts sidebar/inspector width. A view's use of SwiftUI does not establish that its AppKit imports, filesystem access, process host or pointer-only affordances fit mobile. ([D92][D92], [D94][D94], [mobile contract][M-MOBILE], [source matrix below](#6-selective-reuse-matrix).)
3. **Keep content separate from trusted chrome.** Native decisions and Apple-data cards retain their exact target/origin/actions; generated/mini-app content cannot impersonate those controls. Glass remains chrome, content opaque, and Current/Warm does not replace Frosted/Solid.
