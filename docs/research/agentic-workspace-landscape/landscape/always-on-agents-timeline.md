## Shape of the year

- Nov 2025 to Feb 2026: open-source proof of demand. A WhatsApp relay became OpenClaw and Hermes Agent followed; both self-hosted, chat-app-first, user-keys.
- May to Jun 2026: big-lab hosted agents start (Spark, Scout), wired to their own productivity suites.
- Aug 2026: Grok Bot defines the "named bots with their own computer" product; a burst of open-source clones ships within days (Rakazo and OpenBot on 2026-08-13, OpenMausBot repo on launch day).
- Sep to Oct 2026: consumer and enterprise convergence. Muse (free, consumer), Team Bots, Dots, Autopilot, Manus Cue and the enterprise Gemini agent land within five weeks.

## Observations

- The per-agent cloud computer is now table stakes: Dots, Muse, Spark and Grok Bot all give each agent or user a persistent VM with browser and takeover; local-machine access is an optional add-on, not the default ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/), [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/), [Google Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507)).
- Agents are getting first-class identities: own email address ([Grok Bot changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot), 2026-10-11), own Workspace account and directory entry ([Gemini agent](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/welcome-to-gemini-at-work-2026), 2026-10-08), own Slack account ([Dots FAQ](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001529)), own Entra identity for Scout ([TMC, third-party](https://abouttmc.com/blog/microsoft-build-2026-what-the-announcements-means-for-your-business/)). [INFERENCE] Memoshi bots should own addresses and accounts rather than borrow the user's.
- Security architecture is the main differentiator big labs publish: Meta's host-side Sentinel with surrogate credentials and taint tracking, OpenAI's out-of-sandbox Auto-review. Both still shipped incidents within weeks. [INFERENCE] Deterministic, out-of-agent enforcement will be expected by sophisticated buyers.
- Triggers are broadening from cron to events: Gmail-filter and topic monitors (Spark), event-driven background work (Muse), self-scheduled wake-ups (Dots), per-email-tag routines (Grok Bot).
- Multi-agent is moving from "one assistant" to rosters and shared teammates: Grok Bot group chat and Team Bots, the Gemini agent's sub-agents and coworker agents, Dots' stated "teams of dots" future. Consumer products (Muse, Spark, Dots) still ship one agent per user.
- Pricing is converging on subscription tiers with weekly compute or token caps (Muse tokens/week, Gemini 5-hour refresh plus weekly cap, Dots plan allowances), and agent work is metered separately from conversation [INFERENCE from cited plan pages].
- Commerce friction is the next battleground: Amazon blocked Muse; Meta answered with Stripe Link single-use cards, Grok Bot with X Money and Stripe Link. [INFERENCE] Agents that depend on scraping consumer sites face platform risk.
- Brand churn signals the category is unsettled: Clawdbot to Moltbot to OpenClaw in three days, Scout to Autopilot in under four months, Spark to "Task" in under five.
- The open-source wave clones the Grok Bot shape (roster, own computer, routines) but runs on user hardware and subscriptions. [INFERENCE] Memoshi's room to differentiate lies in combining that local control with the identity, permission and trigger depth the big labs now document.
