### 3c. The CH-01 resume outliers (2026-11-16)

Root cause of the two outliers in the 2026-11-04 approval batches: r010 of
[approval-controlled-ten](../../../../evidence/2026-11-04-memoshi-phase0-awlu2ul0/approval-controlled-ten-6c21ff81/results.md)

[resume-outliers README](../../../../evidence/2026-11-16-memoshi-resume-outliers/README.md). Commit `6f22224`, Debug.

- **Where the time was.** Both lives were entirely inside the host process: the driver's
  resume-to-ready measure exceeds the host's own ready `elapsedMs` by at most 9 ms, so neither
  process spawn nor the driver caused them. The daemon-start ready path is Store open, store
  observer, `runtime.start()`, `runtime.resume()` (policy reconcile, `PiDurable.Harness.open`,
  resume decisions, `harness.resume()`), then `modelsList()` and `listChats()`. For an
  approval-waiting conversation there is no model call in it, and the 11-14 recording-proxy
  record for real-50 holds only `/v1/chat/completions` entries — `modelsList`/model discovery did
  not reach the provider on this path. No phase of it is seconds-scale by construction: model
  discovery is bounded (`RuntimeAdapter.resumeDiscoveryLimit`, 15 s, aborted), and there is no
  sleep or retry loop on the path.
- **Non-reproducibility.** On 2026-11-16 the same seeds, kill plans and startup order were rerun
  on this Mac: controlled (`seed 2026110404`, 12 runs) p50 36 / p95 42 / max 42 ms, 12 of 12
  correct; real model (`seed 2026110403`, `qwen3:0.6b`, 10 runs) p50 64 / p95 166 / max 166 ms,

  machine was not idle (1-minute load 18.9–40, 9.3 GB of 10 GB swap in use) and still produced no

  Supporting facts: the same controlled batch's first two host lives cost 23,862 ms and 2,350 ms

  swap-pressure experiment was not run: the Mac was already at 9.3 of 10 GB swap while four

- **Guard.** `RuntimeResumeOpenLatencyTests` (MemoshiCore) reopens a SIGKILL stand-in image at

  One slow reopen is printed, not failed, because that is how a one-off host stall shows. The
  stand-in's own crash-image copy and prior close are recorded but not bounded. The timings
  quoted for the loaded development Mac on 2026-11-16 (ready total p50 18 ms, max 32 ms; store
  open p50 4 ms, start p50 8 ms, resume p50 2 ms) have no evidence record; the test prints its

  loosened.
- **What would prove it further.** The row's pending Release series on the reference box: run the

  the cause to that machine's state; if one appears anywhere, add per-phase elapsed fields to the

  2026-11-04 runs lacked.
