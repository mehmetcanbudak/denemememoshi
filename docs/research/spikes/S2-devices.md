Reports are due Wed 2026-11-25; the gate is Fri 2026-11-27. Results recorded before the `slice-2` pin (`de5e8ee`, 2026-11-14) are on the earlier Pi_Swift development candidate `892585f`, so under [D90](../../plan/decisions.md#d90) they are development evidence; that covers the runs up to `s2-full-04` (`4db72e2`). **What later records changed in the 11-04 draft.**
- The draft carried the 2026-11-03 full local run as authoritative within its scope. The [2026-11-13 code review](../../HANDOFF.md#code-review-2026-11-13) found two weak points in it. Its claim-level and link-level revocation refusals came from the revoked device's own guards, and its expiry two-writer audit checked nothing (`audit.expiry.commits: 0`). [D123](../../plan/decisions.md#d123) therefore treats those points as unproven until a rerun. The rerun on `4db72e2` ([s2-full-04](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/s2-full-04/README.md)) now supplies them: the authority itself refused the revoked device's claim and renewal; that device had held a lease before its removal; the expiry audit has 1,774 commits from 2 writers over 40 epochs, with no epoch holding two writers.
- The draft said real QR expiry and authority load were not selected. Both were selected in s2-full-04. A code used 301 s after issue was refused, and the 12-worker load matrix passed its verifier.
- The quick-cohort failures the draft retained trace to storage stalls on the USB build volume, not to the lease design ([D102](../../plan/decisions.md#d102), [diagnosis](../../../../evidence/2026-11-07-memoshi-engineering/analysis/s2-renewal-diagnosis.md)). Renewals now extend by the claimed TTL ([D104](../../plan/decisions.md#d104)).
- The first full run of 11-13 failed when the 45th device could not pair. That led to [D132](../../plan/decisions.md#d132) (multi-message pairing; at most 64 devices), and the failed record is kept ([s2-full-01](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/s2-full-01/README.md)). The group-state rewrite cost was cut by a journal ([D133](../../plan/decisions.md#d133)). Targets are now measured with 8 devices; the 54-device phase is kept as a stress test.

**Review fixes (2026-11-18).** Device layer in `662ce10`, `s2` driver in `8b9b170`; covered by [s2-full-08](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/s2-full-08/README.md) and [canonical-ci-15](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/canonical-ci-15/README.md) on `a2ac274` ([HANDOFF, deep review](../../HANDOFF.md#deep-review-2026-11-18)).
- Device layer (`Packages/MemoshiIPC/Sources/MemoshiDevice/`, `MemoshiDeviceApple/`):
  - Journal compaction replaces the history through `DeviceStateJournal.replaceHistory(with:)`: a write barrier, a rename on the held directory and a directory sync. After the first compaction a `<file>.replaced` marker is written; a file with that marker and no complete row refuses to open instead of opening empty.
  - Pairing: the joiner accepts the inviter's final hello opened in the add's epoch or a later one; another member's commit no longer drops an owed hello, which the inviter seals again in the current epoch; once the authority accepted the add, the inviter reports success, and if its host's completion of the add fails it reports the pairing as pending (`PairingResult.completionPending`) and the node completes the add on its retry (`Pairing.swift`). That outcome sentence awaits the owner's decision ([HANDOFF §8, proposed decisions of 2026-11-18](../../HANDOFF.md#proposed-decisions-2026-11-18)).
  - The authority refuses a signed add of a 65th device and keeps the epoch; before, it accepted one, which then failed closed at the native roster (`RelayLeaseAuthority.swift`).
  - A send during an unfinished add also resolves an add whose authority reply was lost: the node reads the authority's membership and recovers the pending commit, with the same backoff, so a later send succeeds without `recover()` (`DeviceNode.swift`).
  - A removal moves each lease the removed device held to the next epoch, with no runner and `reason: revoked`, in the same membership record; the first claim after it is granted that epoch, and the grant carries `reason: revoked`. Before, the leases only expired and the epoch moved at the next claim (`RelayLeaseAuthority.swift`, `DeviceNode.swift`). The `s2` driver's revocation check now expects the held epoch plus one (`S2Driver.swift`).
  - Not built: pairing a removed device again as a new device. The host keeps its Keychain identity, so a new identity fails as a duplicate; whether the identity is kept or removed after a verified removal awaits the owner (same list).
  - After a removal the group's transport store is erased too, at `<zone>/transport` (`CloudKitDeviceHost.transportStoreDirectory()`); that location also awaits the owner's decision (same list). `eraseAfterRemoval()` tries every store and then lists every failure (`CloudRemovalErasureFailure`).
  - A seal's own plaintext receipt ends when the message is sealed, and seals are serialized per message ID (`DeviceApplicationTransport.swift`).
  - A link closes when a fragment after the first is refused (`DeviceLink.swift`).
  - `connectRelay` is refused once the device's removal is installed, and `reportRemoval` reads the epoch before it sets its once-flag, so no caller returns while the deletion is unfinished (`DeviceNode.swift`).
  - The relay drops a removed device's mailbox, also for a device revoked while no relay heard it (`Relay.swift`, `RelayMailbox.swift`).
  - A first enrollment removes the files an interrupted attempt left, once `keychain.create()` succeeds (`Packages/MemoshiApp/Sources/AppCore/DevicePairing/DeviceFirstEnrollment.swift`).
  - `OfflineApprovalMailbox.erase()` can be retried after a failure, and the device tests share one event wait bound (`deviceTestEventBound`).
- `s2` driver (`S2Driver.swift`, `S2DeviceProcess.swift`, `S2RelayProcess.swift`): each relay refusal is tied to its own probe through the relay's new `stats` command; a member raw-claim control (`rawClaimAndRelease`) runs in the same batch; the probe waits for `removedFromGroup` with no fixed settle; only fence or membership refusals count as refused writes, in `commitStream` and in `writeLoop`, and any other write failure fails the batch; the scenarios that run once are reported in their own `once` section with their own `groupDevices`. Details: [qualification README](../../../Packages/MemoshiDaemon/Sources/MemoshiQualification/README.md#s2-durable-device-and-authority-qualification-prototype). The s2-full records above predate all of this.

## 6. Spike code

S2 has no `spikes/S2-*` folder. Its code is product code: `Packages/MemoshiIPC/Sources/MemoshiDevice/`, `MemoshiDeviceApple/`, `Packages/MemoshiMLS/` and the `s2` driver in `Packages/MemoshiDaemon/Sources/MemoshiQualification/`. The harness stays ([phase-0 S2](../../plan/phase-0.md#s2), "The harness stays"). It becomes the devices test suite, with simulated clocks and networks added in CI. The three checkers (`check_d123_v2.py`, `check_d133_extra.py`, `verify_s2_samples.py`) and the runner `run-s2-full-v2.py` moved from the [canonical-s2 evidence](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/README.md) into `scripts/` on 2026-11-16 (`59a4ff8`); the runner's process helper is `scripts/lib/owned_process.py`. Since 2026-11-18 (`8b9b170`) the checkers fail closed on absent counters and read the new `once` section, and `scripts/tests/test_s2_checkers.py` tests them; CI's "Release tooling contracts" step runs `scripts/tests` (`scripts/ci.sh`), and [canonical-ci-15](../../../../evidence/2026-11-13-memoshi-canonical-s2/canonical-ci-15/README.md) on `a2ac274` passed its 105 tests. The copies in the evidence folder stay as the versions the recorded runs before `s2-full-08` used.
